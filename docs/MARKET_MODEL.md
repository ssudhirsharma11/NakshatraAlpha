# Nakshatra Alpha Market Model

Version : 1.0

---

# Vision

Nakshatra Alpha is an evidence-based astrological market research framework.

It combines traditional Vedic astrology with historical market data to identify repeatable market behaviour.

Every astrological principle begins as a hypothesis.

Every hypothesis is validated using historical market data.

Rules are continuously updated as new evidence becomes available.

The objective is not to prove astrology correct.

The objective is to discover which principles consistently explain market behaviour.

# Core Philosophy

The market is influenced by multiple astrological layers.

No single layer should be analysed in isolation.

Strong market trends occur when multiple independent
layers point in the same direction.

Conflicting layers reduce trend conviction and increase
the probability of sideways movement.
